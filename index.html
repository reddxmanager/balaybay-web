Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 12, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the mobile applications "Gubbins Gacha", "Hatchbyte", "Spin It", "Fidge It", "One Thing", "Whose Turn?" and "Did I?" developed by ReddX Industries.

General Data Collection

Basic Use: Most of our applications do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) during basic use. No account registration is required to use core features.

Optional Google Sign-In (ReddXAuth)

Some apps ("Spin It", "Did I?", "Fidge It") offer optional Google Sign-In to sync purchased themes across apps. When you choose to sign in:

  • Your Google email address and name are stored via Firebase Authentication
  • Theme purchase records are linked to your account for cross-app access
  • This data is stored securely on Firebase servers
  • You can sign out at any time to stop syncing
  • Signing in is completely optional - apps work without it

Did I? - Reminder Notifications

The "Did I?" app uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver reminder notifications. This requires:

  • A randomly generated device identifier (not linked to your identity)
  • A push notification token provided by Google
  • Your device's timezone offset
  • Reminder times and labels you configure

This data is stored securely on Firebase servers and is used solely to deliver your scheduled reminders.

In-App Purchases

Some apps offer Pro features via Google Play billing or Gumroad license keys. Purchase transactions are handled by Google Play or Gumroad - we do not collect or store payment information. Only purchase confirmation data (product ID, purchase token) is stored to verify entitlements.

Data Sharing and Security

We do not share your data with third parties except as required to provide app functionality (Firebase for authentication/notifications, Google Play for purchases). All data is transmitted over secure HTTPS connections and stored using industry-standard security practices.

Data Deletion

You can delete your data by:

  • Uninstalling the app (deletes local data immediately)
  • Signing out of Google (stops syncing, data remains for recovery)
  • Contacting us to request full account deletion

Contact: For questions or data deletion requests, contact us at reddxmanager@gmail.com
